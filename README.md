Customer Segmentation using RFM Analysis

Project Overview

This project uses RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Analysis to segment customers based on their purchasing behavior. The analysis helps businesses identify High Value Customers, At Risk Customers, and New Customers for targeted marketing and retention strategies.

Dataset

Retail Sales Dataset

- 120,000 transaction records
- Customer purchase history
- Sales and transaction information

Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

Project Workflow

1. Data Loading and Exploration
2. Data Cleaning
3. RFM Analysis
4. Customer Segmentation
5. Data Visualization
6. Revenue Analysis by Customer Segment
7. Top 5 Customer Identification
8. Business Insights and Recommendations

Customer Segments

- High Value Customers
- New Customers
- At Risk Customers

Visualizations

- Customer Segment Distribution (Bar Chart)
- Customer Segment Distribution (Pie Chart)
- Top 5 Customers Analysis

Key Insights

- High Value customers contribute significantly to overall revenue.
- At Risk customers have not purchased recently and require re-engagement strategies.
- Top customers play an important role in overall sales performance.
- Customer segmentation helps businesses create targeted marketing campaigns.
- Revenue analysis provides valuable insights into customer behavior.

Business Recommendations

1. Implement loyalty programs and exclusive rewards for High Value customers.
2. Run personalized campaigns and special offers for At Risk customers.
3. Provide welcome offers and product recommendations for New Customers.

Conclusion

RFM Analysis is an effective customer segmentation technique that helps businesses understand customer behavior, improve customer retention, and increase revenue through data-driven decision making.
